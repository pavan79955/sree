# 👑 Birthday Princess Magic - Mobile Web Application ✨

A romantic, heartfelt, and interactive mobile-first web application designed to give your special person an unforgettable birthday experience.

---

## ✨ Features Crafted Especially for Her

### 🔐 1. Birthday Gate / Secret Date Login
- **Passcode Protection**: She logs in by selecting her exact **Birth Month, Day, and Year**.
- **Delightful Feedback**: Plays a golden chime and showers the screen in confetti when she unlocks it!
- **Clue & Quick Preview**: A "Need a clue?" prompt helps if she wants a hint, plus a "Quick Preview" shortcut for testing.

### 💌 2. Royal Greeting & Wax-Sealed Love Letter
- **Romantic Heading**: Displays her name in handwritten calligraphy with floating crowns and sparkles.
- **Interactive Wax Seal**: Tap the crimson wax seal with realistic breaking sound to unfold a vintage parchment letter.
- **Live Typewriter Effect**: The letter types out smoothly word by word just like a handwritten note.

### 🎂 3. Interactive Birthday Cake & Wishing Ceremony
- **Tiered Strawberry & Frosting Cake**: Handcrafted CSS illustration with warm glowing candlelight.
- **Blow Out the Candles**: Tap the candles or the *"Blow Out Candles"* button. The flames extinguish with realistic smoke animations, celebratory chimes, and a massive confetti explosion!
- **Star Wish Card**: A heartfelt wish card drops down from the stars.

### 🎵 4. Birthday Song (Spotify)
- The Spotify player (with song cover) appears at the bottom and **starts from the first line** when she blows out the candles. Needs internet; full song needs Spotify login in the browser.

### 🎼 5. Dreamy Music Box (fallback) (Zero External Dependencies)
- Built with the browser's native **Web Audio API**.
- Plays a gentle, romantic music-box melody of *"Happy Birthday to You"* on loop without needing external MP3 files.
- Interactive sound visualizer equalizer bars in the top bar.

### 🌸 6. Ambient Petals & Stardust Canvas
- Floating sakura blossom petals, glowing pink hearts, and stardust float in 60fps.
- Touching or tapping anywhere on the screen creates a gentle burst of sparkling fairy dust.
